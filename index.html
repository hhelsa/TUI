The Idea

Inclusive growth does not happen by accident — it is organised. TUI places citizens at the centre of an accountable, cooperative economy, connecting collective effort to enterprise, opportunity and shared prosperity.

“An enterprising, prosperous, peaceful & happy society.” — TUI's aspiration

The Ecosystem

Not one programme.
One connected system.

TUI connects cooperatives, youth and collective enterprises to the finance, technology, knowledge and networks they need — so wealth creation is inclusive, and locally led.

    How we build capability

    Business Development

    Tailor-made cooperative training, organisational development, mentorship and coaching — delivered through SkillCoop and #NoExcuses, reinforced by business networking that keeps cooperatives connected.

    • SkillCoop
    • #NoExcuses
    • Coaching & Mentorship
    • Business Networking
    02

    How we fund opportunity

    Financing

    Through Fanaka Finance, TUI delivers credit, agency management, fund management and financial literacy to cooperative MSMEs — Piki Credit for transport cooperatives, Soko Credit for market traders, Kilimo Credit for agricultural marketing.

    • Fanaka Finance
    • Piki Credit
    • Soko Credit
    • Kilimo Credit

    Piki Credit was first piloted with the Kampala Civic Center Boda-Boda Transporters Cooperative Society.
